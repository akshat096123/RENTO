# RENTO — COMPLETE PROJECT OVERVIEW

## 1. PROJECT NAME

**RENTO**

### Tagline

**“Don’t Buy. Request. Rent. Return.”**

RENTO is an **AI-powered, demand-first rental marketplace** that allows people to rent products from individuals, businesses, rental stores, photographers, event companies, students, and other verified suppliers.

The platform is not limited to products that are already listed.

If a user searches for something and:

- cannot find it,
- does not find a suitable product,
- does not like the available options,
- cannot find it within their budget,
- or needs a specific specification,

they can create a **custom rental request**.

That request is then intelligently distributed to relevant registered owners/suppliers who may have the required product.

The platform combines:

- Rental marketplace
- AI-powered product identification
- AI requirement understanding
- AI product suitability matching
- AI authenticity screening
- AI condition analysis
- Demand-driven supply discovery
- Owner-to-renter matching
- Secure communication
- Buffered/escrow-style payment flow
- Third-party logistics integration
- Delivery tracking
- Digital handover verification
- Security deposits
- AI-assisted damage detection
- Dispute management
- Two-sided ratings
- AI pricing recommendations
- AI demand prediction
- Fraud/risk detection

---

# 2. CORE PROBLEM

Traditional rental platforms mainly work like this:

**Owner lists product → Customer searches → Customer rents**

This creates a major problem.

If the product is not already listed, the customer simply sees:

> “No results found.”

The customer may then have to:

- search another website,
- contact multiple rental shops,
- ask friends,
- use social media,
- or buy the product instead.

RENTO solves this through a **demand-first marketplace**.

Instead of saying:

> “This product isn't listed.”

RENTO says:

> **“Tell us what you need. We'll try to find someone who can provide it.”**

The customer request itself becomes a way of discovering hidden/unlisted supply.

# 13. AI AUTHENTICITY SCREENING

RENTO should include an **AI-powered authenticity screening system**.

Important: The AI should not claim that it can guarantee a product is 100% genuine from an image alone.

Instead, it provides an **Authenticity Confidence / Risk Assessment**.

AI can examine:

- Product appearance
- Brand markings
- Model information
- Serial number information where applicable
- Packaging
- Product specifications
- Documentation
- Uploaded photographs
- Listing consistency
- Price anomalies
- Image manipulation
- Claimed model vs detected model

Possible results:

### 🟢 Low Risk

Authenticity confidence high.

### 🟡 Verification Required

More information/manual verification required.

### 🔴 High Risk

Potential inconsistency or suspicious listing.

High-value products can require additional manual/KYC verification.

The RENTO platform manages the rental transaction and connects the logistics system to it.

**RENTO is an AI-powered demand-first rental marketplace that connects people who need products temporarily with verified individuals and businesses that can supply them, even when the product is not already listed, while providing intelligent matching, product verification, secure buffered payments, third-party logistics, delivery tracking, and AI-assisted condition verification.**

---

# 50. SHORT PITCH

**RENTO is a demand-driven rental marketplace built around one simple idea: if a product isn't available, the user shouldn't have to give up. They can describe what they need, and AI converts that requirement into a structured request and connects it with relevant owners and businesses. AI also identifies uploaded products, evaluates their suitability and authenticity risk, recommends pricing, and helps detect condition changes. Once a product is selected, the renter pays through a secure buffered payment system. A third-party logistics provider handles transportation, and the payment remains protected until the required delivery milestone is verified. After the rental, AI compares before-and-after condition, deposits are released, and both sides build reputation through ratings.**

- Verification

For:
